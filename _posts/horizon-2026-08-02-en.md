## [F*: A Proof-Oriented Programming Language](https://fstar-lang.org/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

F* is a general-purpose, proof-oriented programming language designed for formal verification, with community feedback highlighting its potential and areas for improvement. F* provides a powerful tool for ensuring the correctness and security of software, which is crucial in critical systems such as cryptographic protocols and operating system kernels. F* supports dependent types, monadic effects, and refinement types, allowing precise specifications for programs. It can be translated to OCaml, F#, C, WebAssembly, or assembly language.

**Background**: Formal verification is the process of proving or disproving the correctness of a system with respect to a certain formal specification or property using formal methods of mathematics. F* is a joint project of Microsoft Research and Inria, introduced in 2011 and under active development on GitHub.
