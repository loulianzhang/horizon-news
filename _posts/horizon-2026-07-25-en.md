## [Android May Soon Restrict On-Device ADB](https://kitsumed.github.io/blog/posts/android-may-soon-restrict-on-device-adb/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Android is considering implementing restrictions on on-device ADB, which could impact how developers and users interact with their devices. This change could affect the convenience and flexibility of using ADB for development and debugging, potentially impacting a wide range of users and developers who rely on this tool. The proposed restrictions aim to enhance security, but they may also limit the functionality of tools like Shizuku and libadb. Some community members argue that the attack vector is not realistic for most users.

hackernews · shscs911 · Jul 25, 06:57 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49045159)

**Background**: Android Debug Bridge (ADB) is a versatile command-line tool that allows communication between a computer and an Android device. It is commonly used for installing and debugging apps, and it provides access to a Unix shell. On-device ADB refers to the use of ADB directly on the Android device itself, without the need for a separate developer machine.
