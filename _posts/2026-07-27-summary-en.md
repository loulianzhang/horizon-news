## [Investigation into LLM Token Reselling and Fraud](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/26/relay-market/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Matt Lenhard conducted an investigation into the market for reselling LLM tokens at a discount, often through fraudulent means, using open-source proxy software like one-api and new-api. This investigation highlights the security and fraud risks in the AI/ML space, particularly in China, where resellers exploit free trials, unprotected support bots, and stolen credit cards to offer discounted access to LLMs. The open-source software used for these proxies includes one-api and its more actively developed fork, new-api, which can be used to load balance requests across a pool of API credentials. Buyers are seeking cheap tokens, avoiding geo-restrictions, and sometimes collecting data for model distillation.

rss · Simon Willison · Jul 26, 19:30

**Background**: LLM tokens are the fundamental units that large language models process. When you type a prompt into an LLM, the text is first broken into tokens by a tokenizer. The market for reselling these tokens has grown, often through fraudulent means, to provide cheaper access to LLMs.
