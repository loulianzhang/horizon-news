## [No Leap Second in December 2026](https://datacenter.iers.org/data/latestVersion/bulletinC.txt) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service (IERS) has announced that no leap second will be introduced at the end of December 2026. This decision impacts global timekeeping and ensures that systems relying on precise time synchronization, such as computer networks and satellite operations, remain stable. Leap seconds are occasionally added to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) to account for the Earth's rotational irregularities. The last leap second was added on December 31, 2016.

hackernews · ChrisArchitect · Jul 9, 14:16 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48846281)

**Background**: The IERS is responsible for maintaining global time and reference frame standards. Leap seconds are used to keep UTC in sync with observed solar time (UT1), which varies due to the Earth's rotational slowdown. Since 1972, 27 leap seconds have been added to UTC.
