## [Exploring Colors Beyond Screen Capabilities](https://moultano.wordpress.com/2026/06/19/where-to-find-the-colors-your-screen-cant-show-you/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The article delves into the limitations of color reproduction on screens and highlights where to find colors that cannot be displayed, with community comments adding valuable perspectives on color perception and technology. This topic is significant for software engineers, especially in graphics and UI/UX design, as it highlights the importance of understanding color gamut and human color perception in creating more realistic and engaging visual experiences. The sRGB color space, which is still widely used, has limitations in reproducing many saturated orange, red, and purple colors. The CIE 1931 chromaticity diagram, while useful, overemphasizes certain areas of the color spectrum that are less distinguishable by human vision.

hackernews · moultano · Jun 20, 03:36 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48606140)

**Background**: Color gamut refers to the range of colors that can be accurately represented by a device or within a defined color space. Human color perception is based on the three types of cone cells in the eye, each sensitive to different wavelengths of light. The brain combines the information from these cells to create the perception of color.
